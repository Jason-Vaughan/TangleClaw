5. Sprint Post-Mortem: What Worked vs. What Didn’t (A475 & Obsr)

Candid synthesis by the Project Manager, Architect (A475), Integrator (TC-Lane-INT), and Sprint Observer (TC-Lane-Obsr) of what succeeded and the concrete process/tooling bottlenecks uncovered during v5.40.0.

✅ What Worked Exceptionally Well (Keep & Codify)

  • 1. Rule #275.3 Headless Chrome + Node 22 Native WebSocket CDP Caught a Real 403 OPEN_INSTALL_TOKEN_INVALID Bug Before Merge (W2-C #2637) Rather than blocking on browser-extension loopback limits, TC-Lane-C spawned headless Google Chrome (--headless=new --remote-debugging-port=9637) inside the Builder shell and drove it via Node 22 WebSocket CDP across 1280x800, 375x667, and 320x640 viewports. That live rendered check caught and fixed a real 403 OPEN_INSTALL_TOKEN_INVALID bug in public/session.js before PR #2637 merged.
  • 2. P0 Bundle Cap (≤3 Issues/Car) + Non-Interactive Execution Eliminated All AskUserQuestion TUI Freezes (59.3m Mean Build Time) Across all 8 Builder cars in Waves 1 and 2, capping cars at 1–3 issues and forbidding interactive AskUserQuestion TUI prompts achieved a 59.3m mean build time (20.7m on W2-D, 36.3m on W1-C, 44.8m on W2-B, 52.0m on W1-A) with zero pane-no-prompt TUI stalls.
  • 3. P1 (PR #2633) Paid Off Inside the Same Sprint on W3-REC (15/15 Newly Cited Tests: Anchors Validated) Shipped by TC-Lane-D in Wave 2 in just 20.7m build time, P1 updated scripts/verify-features-index.js to accept existing on-disk test files in docs/records-only diffs — allowing TC-Lane-INT’s W3-REC branch to retain all 15 newly cited Tests: anchors in FEATURES.md (47/47 passing in test/features-index.test.js).
  • 4. SINGLE-TURN PLAN+BUILD in Wave 2 Eliminated Mid-Wave /clear Overhead Authorizing SINGLE-TURN PLAN+BUILD for Wave 2 allowed Builders A/B/C/D to plan, obtain Integrator scope sign-off, implement, and open their PRs in a single continuous turn without an intermediate /clear between plan approval and coding.

⚠️ What Didn’t Work & Proven Fixes for v5.41.0

  • 1. Sequential SUITE_SLOT Ancestor-Sync Rule Forced 7 Redundant Rebases & CI/Review Cycles Across Disjoint Wave 1 & 2 Cars (Finding W1-F1_v540) Even though Wave 1 cars were 100% file-disjoint, requiring every sibling PR to force-sync onto the latest main after each merge forced W1-A (PR #2629, built in 52.0m) through 2 ancestor syncs and 3 full CI + PRR + A-Gate cycles, stretching its open-to-merge time to 194.5m (3h 14m). In Wave 2, W2-B, W2-A, and W2-C similarly had to re-sync after each sibling landed. Fix for v5.41.0: When a wave charter certifies zero file overlap and git merge-tree is clean, merge already-approved sibling PRs back-to-back without invalidating prior exact-head CI/PRR/A-Gate approvals.
  • 2. 10 Review Rounds on W2-C (PR #2637) Due to Trust-Boundary & Session-Launch Wiring Iterations (209.4m Open-to-Merge) Combining durable scoped permission requests (#2352), TCC tmux bootstrap (#2625), and session launch wiring (#2636) required 10 review rounds between TC-Lane-C, PRR-B, and Architect (`A475-W2C-TRUST`). Fix for v5.41.0: Pre-specify exact trust-boundary invariants (`A475-W2C-TRUST` equivalent) inside the initial Wave Charter before Builder dispatch whenever a car touches permission/auth or launch-identity wiring.
  • 3. Claude-in-Chrome Extension Loopback Blocker Forced Browser-Check Deferral on W2-A (PR #2635, A475-W2A-R6) While TC-Lane-C succeeded with direct headless Chrome + Node 22 WebSocket CDP on #2637, TC-Lane-A hit the Claude-in-Chrome extension loopback/sandbox blocker on public/operator-bridge-panel.js, requiring Architect deferral A475-W2A-R6 to W4-REL post-deploy verification. Fix for v5.41.0: Codify TC-Lane-C’s headless Chrome + Node 22 WebSocket CDP script as a shared helper (scripts/with-headless-chrome-cdp.js) in all Builder rules so no UI PR ever depends on the browser extension.
  • 4. 100.9m Merge-Slot Gap on W1-D (PR #2631) & 120m Coordinator Workload Receipt Expiry (Finding W1-F2_v540) W1-D’s exact-head CI passed at 04:48:32Z, but merge execution waited until 06:29:26Z (`100.9m`), while TC-Lane-PM and TC-Lane-Obsr crossed the 120m waiting-external receipt TTL. Fix for v5.41.0: Auto-wake TC-Lane-INT when all 3 merge predicates (CI + PRR + A-Gate) turn green on the current head, and extend coordinator waiting-external TTL to 240m.
