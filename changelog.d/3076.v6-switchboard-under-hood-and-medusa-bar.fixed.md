- The v6 chat no longer shows `[TangleClaw Switchboard]` wake notices as messages from the operator (#3076). A wake is typed into the session's pane, so it read as a prompt; recorded and on-screen wakes now leave the conversation and are listed Under the Hood, under "Switchboard & Medusa traffic", together with every `tc message send` the session's pane shows. The chat header also gains a Medusa indicator: the inbound (left) and outbound (right) heads blink when traffic moves, and the `@` badge becomes the unread count. Its tip summarises the listener's state and the traffic, and clicking it opens Under the Hood at that list. It reads the listener status (which marks nothing read), never blinks for mail that was already waiting, and is hidden for a project that has not turned Medusa on.
