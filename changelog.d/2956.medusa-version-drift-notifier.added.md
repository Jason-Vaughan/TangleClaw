- Added a Medusa upstream & local Hub drift check, pull and push, with a dashboard
  notice: `lib/medusa-drift.js` compares the local `Jason-Vaughan/Medusa` checkout's
  HEAD against its own `origin/main` (`updateAvailable`) and the running Hub's
  reported `gitSha` against that checkout's disk HEAD (`hubRestartNeeded`),
  tolerating a Hub that predates `Jason-Vaughan/Medusa#70`'s `gitSha`/`capabilities`
  fields. Checked ~1 minute after startup and every 30 minutes after, exposed at
  `GET /api/system/medusa-drift` (`?refresh=1` forces a fresh, fetch-first check),
  and refreshed immediately on a `hub.version_updated` WebSocket frame
  (`lib/medusa-listener.js`, fanned out via `lib/medusa.js`'s new
  `onHubVersionUpdated`/`applyHubVersionUpdate`) without waiting for the next poll.
  The Dashboard shows a read-only warning chip when either condition holds, naming
  what to do; a one-click Operator action to fast-forward the checkout and restart
  `com.medusa.hub` is deferred to #2967 (#2956).
