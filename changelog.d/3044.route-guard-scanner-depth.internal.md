- The master release-qualification suite's route-guard scanner now counts a fail-closed caller check as an `/api/v6` route's guard only at the handler's top level (#3044). A check on one branch (with or without braces), or inside a callback that may never run, no longer passes an otherwise unguarded handler, and before the guard the scanner now flags a request-body property read as well as a body-helper call, and a call in any spelling (`(x)()`, `o[k]()`, `x?.()`, `new X`, a tagged template). The guard's own arguments, which run before it, must be exactly `(req, res, '<action>')`. Mutation regressions cover each case. The malformed-fragment assertion is renamed to say what it checks.
