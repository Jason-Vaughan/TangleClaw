- The master release-qualification suite's route-guard scanner now counts a fail-closed caller check as an `/api/v6` route's guard only at the handler's top level (#3044). A check on one branch (with or without braces), or inside a callback that may never run, no longer passes an otherwise unguarded handler, and reading a request-body property before the guard is flagged too, not only calling a body helper. Mutation regressions cover each case. The malformed-fragment assertion is renamed to say what it checks.
