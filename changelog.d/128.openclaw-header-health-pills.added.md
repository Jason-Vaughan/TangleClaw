- Added system resource and health pills to the OpenClaw session header. TangleClaw detects whether the wrapped OpenClaw gateway is Dockerized or host-installed and surfaces CPU%, memory%, and pathology signals (stuck sessions, lock holds over max, container health) as a color-coded pill (`pill-ok`/`pill-warn`/`pill-alarm`), with a recent-issue history dot that stays lit for an hour after the last unhealthy reading even once the gateway recovers. Click either pill to see the detail in the existing sidecar panel. This car ships the detection helper and the pure renderer; wiring a live polling route is left to a follow-up car, since `lib/routes/openclaw.js` and `server.js` are outside this car's file envelope (#128).
