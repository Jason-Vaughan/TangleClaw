- Medusa messaging now stays inside the caller's fleet (#3077). A project's fleet is its project groups plus its tags. Two projects share a fleet when they share a group or a tag (tags compare ignoring case and spaces), and projects with no group and no tag share one default pool, so an install that uses no groups works as before. A grouped project and an ungrouped one never share a fleet. A project's send outside its fleet is refused with `403 CROSS_FLEET_FORBIDDEN` before anything reaches the Hub, unless the send carries `allowCrossFleet: true`. A grouped or tagged project also cannot reach a workspace that belongs to no local project without that flag. A project's roster lists its own fleet plus the Master (`?scope=all` lists every fleet). A coordinator project's broadcast reaches its own fleet by default, and can be narrowed with `targets`. Reaching every fleet needs `scope: "all"` with `emergency: true` or `confirmAllFleets: true`, or it is refused with `403 BROADCAST_ALL_REQUIRES_EMERGENCY`. A startup announcement only wakes coordinators in the announcing project's fleet. The Operator and the Master are exempt throughout, and the Master and the operator bridge stay reachable from every fleet, so a project can always answer the Operator.
