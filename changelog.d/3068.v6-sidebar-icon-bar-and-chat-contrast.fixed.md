- The v6 Fleet sidebar's header is now a row of four icons with hover tips (#3068): `«` hides the sidebar, `❖` is the fleet view (the only mode today), `🔍` opens search and the engine filter, and `🕓` is reserved for recent chats (not active yet). The group pills, the Multi toggle and the Expand all and Collapse all buttons are gone: a single fleet picker (All Fleets or one fleet) chooses what the tree shows, each project and fleet keeps its own fold toggle, and a selection of several fleets saved by the old Multi mode still applies and shows as "Several fleets". Closing the search clears its text and engine filter, so a filter you cannot see never hides projects. The chat window's header, messages, Under the Hood drawer and message box, and the sidebar's edge, have thicker, higher-contrast outlines and a filled surface (with a lighter outline in the light theme).
