- Session-lifecycle mutation routes (launch, kill, the four `/wrap*` routes) now independently
  enforce caller identity instead of relying on the generic launch-binding floor plus caller
  self-restraint: launch and kill admit only the operator or the Project Master (never a
  project's own session, including itself), a wrap admits the operator, the Master, or the
  project's own current session acting on itself (never another project's), and the Master's
  calls additionally pass through its own lifecycle-authority gate (#2132/#966) (#2129).
