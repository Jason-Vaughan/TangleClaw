- The v6 shell shows a usage meter for Claude, Codex and Antigravity in its footer (#3085, the shell half). Each engine's bar is green, amber at 25% left or less, and red and pulsing at 5% or less, using the lowest "% left" a live session reports and otherwise the operator's calibration. Clicking the meter opens a breakdown with each engine's 5-hour, weekly and sprint token totals, its live sessions, the time to the weekly reset, and a one-click "% left" calibration for each engine.
