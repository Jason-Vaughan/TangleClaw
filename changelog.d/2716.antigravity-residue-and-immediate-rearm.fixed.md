- Clearing a stranded Medusa switchboard nudge from an Antigravity session's
  prompt now works: `C-u` is intercepted by Antigravity's top-level TUI
  (scrolling its message viewport) before it ever reaches the composer, so
  the clear silently did nothing and every retry aborted with
  `stranded-nudge-residue`. Antigravity sessions are now sent `C-a C-k`
  instead, which reaches the composer and clears the same way `C-u` does for
  every other engine.
- A wake nudge the engine proves it did not accept now re-arms immediately,
  rather than waiting up to 30 seconds for the delivery watchdog's own
  periodic timer plus another two 5-second poll ticks before the retry is
  even attempted. The first `wake_not_accepted` receipt now triggers the
  watchdog's durable re-arm check for that recipient right away, and asks the
  wake monitor to look again almost immediately when something was re-armed
  — both the budget on how many times an exchange may be re-armed and the
  backoff between re-arms are unchanged (#2716).
- That "look again almost immediately" request is now honored rather than
  silently dropped. A request landing too soon after the wake monitor's own
  last observation of the pane (within its minimum observation window) used
  to be discarded outright, leaving the retry to wait for the monitor's next
  ordinary timer pass regardless — exactly the delay the immediate re-arm
  above exists to avoid. It is now scheduled for the earliest moment it can
  safely run instead, no later than the monitor's own window requires and no
  sooner (#2716).
