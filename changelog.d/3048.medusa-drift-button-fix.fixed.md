- The Medusa update chip (`#medusaDriftNotice`) no longer looks dead when clicked (#3048). It used to ask through the browser's own `confirm()`, which some browsers and webviews suppress, so a click did nothing at all; it now confirms on the chip itself ("Click again to apply the Medusa update", within 10 seconds). A refusal or failure now stays on the chip, with a short reason in its label and the full reason in its tooltip, instead of reverting to "Medusa update available". The update itself no longer refuses for untracked files or `.tangleclaw/` runtime state in the Medusa checkout, and a refusal names the changed files and the current branch. On macOS, a Hub restart is refused, with the running Hub left alone, when the `com.medusa.hub` LaunchAgent's `WorkingDirectory` does not exist or its plist cannot be read: `launchctl kickstart -k` would stop the Hub and launchd could not start it again.
