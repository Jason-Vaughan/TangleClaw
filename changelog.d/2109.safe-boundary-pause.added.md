- A session with standing over another lane (its own dispatcher, or the Architect — anyone listed in that lane's `authority.hold`) can now request a safe-boundary pause on it, `tc control pause <project>` (`POST /api/control/pause`), without resorting to a destructive kill. The pause commits a durable HOLD first, then attempts one best-effort direct pane nudge; a busy or unreachable pane escalates instead of retrying, and the HOLD already blocks the target's next governed mutation regardless of whether the nudge lands. Never touches the worktree, never falls back to killing the session.
- Every pane nudge AND every escalation attempt is now recorded durably as a `notify_attempted` receipt against the HOLD's own event — including `no-recipient` when no project principal is configured for escalation, so the operator's dashboard has something to show for a pause whose nudge and escalation both went nowhere (PRR-B Round 2: neither case left a record before this). `GET /api/control/assignments/:id`/`/mine` now also carry `assignment.pauseTimeout` (`{acked, ageMs, timedOut}`, `null` outside a peer-pause HOLD) — a read-only helper answering whether the current pause has gone unacknowledged past a threshold, for a dashboard or a future sweep to act on.
