- The launch domain of the store moved out of `lib/store.js` unchanged into two modules: `lib/store/launch.js` (launch sequences and their step ids, the project recovery state, the recovery clearances and their batches) and `lib/store/handoffs.js` (handoff publications, the per-project handoff epoch and its missing-row reason code). The facade binds both from `init()`, hands the launch module the sessions vocabulary it filters on, and re-exports the same names; `lib/store/sessions.js` now requires the two directly instead of receiving them through its binding, which shrinks to the handle (#2248 T04, Part of #2248).
