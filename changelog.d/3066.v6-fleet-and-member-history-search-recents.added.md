- The v6 Fleet sidebar's `🕓` button now opens a chat history and recents panel (#3066). With no search it lists the chat member's recent saved sessions, newest first; a search looks through wrap summaries or full transcripts, either for that member or for every member of its fleet at once, and names the member and session of each match, best match first (a member whose history cannot be read is named rather than hiding the rest). Opening a result shows that session read-only — its wrap summary and, for a search, the transcript lines that match — with a **Reference in chat** button that adds `[member session #id · date] snippet` to the message you are writing. The panel follows the chat when you switch to another member. Renaming also asks before a rename that would leave a member's Claude or Codex engine state behind: the server's warning is shown with a **Rename anyway** button, and a refused rename keeps the name you typed.
