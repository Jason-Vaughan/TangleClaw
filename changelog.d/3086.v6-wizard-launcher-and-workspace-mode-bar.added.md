- The v6 shell gains a workspace mode bar and an always-available wizard launcher (#3086). Tabs at the top of the main view switch between 💬 Chat, 📋 Sprint & Cars, 🧭 Charter & Roadmap, 📈 Kaizen & Reports and 🧙 Wizard; the arrow keys, Home and End move between them, and opening a project or member always lands in Chat. Sprint & Cars shows the dispatch board from `/api/sprint-waves`, Charter & Roadmap the planned waves, and Kaizen & Reports the running version's sprint report, each drawn by the sprint UI when it is loaded and by a plain escaped list otherwise; a server that does not serve those routes yet says so. A "+ New Project / Fleet Wizard" button in the sidebar and a "+ New" button beside the tabs open the template wizard at any time, even with a chat open, and switching modes leaves the chat, its draft and its scroll as they were. A member's engine badge in the sidebar now shows the engine its live session is running, else the one its last session ran, else the configured one, and its tooltip names the model when one is known (#3094, the shell half).
