Section 01 · Architectural Deep-Dive

How v5.46.1 Restores 100% Local Suite Determinism & Fail-Closed Release Authority

During v5.46.0, cars touching lib/store.js or server.js required two manual Operator local-suite waivers (WAIVE2692 and WAIVE2695) because five distinct environment, process-tree, and concurrency issues broke local macOS test runs. Sprint v5.46.1 systematically root-caused and eliminated every single blocker while hardening group release-ownership fail-closed semantics across all five store, route, wrap, and UI call sites.

PILLAR 01 · WATCHDOG PROCESS-TREE SAFETY & SYSPOLICYD BYPASS (#2691, #2688)

SIGSTOP Freeze-Capture-Kill & Shared Dispatcher Symlinks

W1-D+A (#2705) uncovered why test/soak-guest.test.js appeared to hang in Stage 5 and fixed two subtle signal races in deploy/soak/guest/guest-setup.sh#bounded: (1) killing child processes before the parent shell allowed the shell to wake up past its dead foreground sleep and exit with code 1 instead of timeout 124; (2) killing the parent first caused the kernel to immediately reparent living children to PID 1, causing pkill -P one line later to miss them and leak orphaned sleep processes (caught by TC-Lane-PRR-B). The definitive fix freezes the timed-out command with SIGSTOP first so it cannot exit, resume, or reparent children, captures its child PIDs while frozen, and kills parent and children together in one atomic step (covered by an ESRCH orphan regression test). Simultaneously, replacing ~28 per-test copied fake scripts (~5,800 fresh executables per run, each paying a ~230ms macOS Gatekeeper/syspolicyd first-execve scan) with symlinks to one shared dispatcher inode (~10ms reused) cut runtime from ~804s to ~270s (−66.4%). Finally, #2688 reclassified 5 transitive-subprocess tests (including newly discovered test/session-rules-channel.test.js) from Medium to Large in test/tiers.json and .testament.toml, and added per-tier --test-timeout handling in scripts/test-stage.js#tierArgv.

PILLAR 02 · DARWIN NON-LOGIN PATH, BUNDLED ENGINE SYNC & LOAD DETERMINISM (#2696, #2697, #2698)

Hermetic Setup Fixtures, /usr/sbin/sysctl Fallback & Template Cloning

W1-B (#2704) fixed two macOS host-environment failures: in #2696, test/setup-wizard.test.js’s withNoEngines and withNoEnginesAndStubShell fixtures emptied tmpDir/engines, only for refreshDetectionPath() in lib/engines.js to unconditionally call store.engines.syncBundled() and re-copy claude.json and codex.json back in; suppressing bundled reseeding inside the no-engine fixture restored hermeticity whether or not developer CLIs are installed on the host. In #2697, lib/ttyd-watcher.js#_sysctl and test/ttyd-watcher.test.js failed with ENOENT under non-login shells where PATH omits /usr/sbin; adding a fallback to /usr/sbin/sysctl made Darwin PTY pool introspection PATH-independent. In W1-C (#2703, #2698), test/release-certification-host-publish.test.js eliminated 2×-per-test git init --bare subprocess storms by copying a once-initialized bare repo template, while test/soak-driver.test.js replaced a hardcoded PID 999999 dead-process sentinel with a targeted isAlive stub that marks only the initial stale marker PID dead while keeping live contender PIDs alive.

PILLAR 03 · FAIL-CLOSED GROUP RELEASE AUTHORITY ACROSS 5 CALL SITES (#2699)

Unified safeFindReleaseOwnerElsewhere & Modal Race Guard

W1-D (#2705) resolved all six Critic R5 follow-ups from v5.46.0 PR #2695: (1) lib/store.js’s addMember now checks safeFindReleaseOwnerElsewhere(projectId) before claimReleaseOwnerIfVacant, so joining a second group never auto-claims release ownership when the project already defers to another group’s owner or when the lookup fails (`R-1/R-2`); (2) lib/projects.js threads the real elsewhere boolean into resolveReleaseMode (`R-4`) and fails CLOSED in _applyProjectUpdates when safeFindReleaseOwnerElsewhere returns unresolvable: true, sharing one canonical helper across all 5 call sites (lib/projects.js validator, _applyProjectUpdates, enrichProject, lib/wrap-steps/version-bump.js, and lib/wrap-steps/_release-recommendation.js; `R-5`); (3) public/ui.js prioritizes a conflicting external group owner over self-owned groups in the Project Settings hint (`R-3`) and guards public/ui.js#toggleGroupMembership against stale groupEditId mutations across the post-mutation await api('/api/project-groups') boundary (`R-6`).
