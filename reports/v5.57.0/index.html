📖 Plain-English Summary — What Sprint v5.57.0 Accomplished & Why It Matters Written for operators, stakeholders & non-specialist readers

Transparent Session Stop Provenance, Safe-Boundary Peer Pause, Durable Decision Routing & Codex Transcript Capture

When a 10-lane autonomous coding fleet runs for hours across multiple software releases, operators and coordinators need clear answers to three practical questions: Why did a session stop? How do we safely pause a runaway or conflicting lane without killing its work? And what happens when a lane needs a decision from an Architect or PM whose session is busy or due for a context refresh? Sprint v5.57.0 (“Session stops and decisions”, Leg 1 of our 5-version autonomous sprint train) answered all three across 8 shipped issues and 7 pull requests (+6,833 / -681 LOC):

1. Session Stop Provenance (`#2249`), Explicit Recovery Wake Batch (`#2250`) & Safe Peer Pause (`#2109`)

TangleClaw now records and displays how and why a prior session stopped (`stoppedBy`: `api`, `model-switch`, `wrap`, or `crash`, plus `stopReason`) and lets the operator wake multiple recovery-cleared launches in one explicit action (`POST /api/launch/recovery-wake-batch`). Even better, coordinators with standing over a lane can now pause it at a safe boundary (`tc control pause `, `#2109`)—committing a durable HOLD, recording every nudge/escalation receipt, and escalating automatically on timeout without ever killing the session or touching its worktree.

2. Durable Decision Routing (`#2037`), Unreachable-Decider Fallback (`#2351`) & Governed Refresh (`#2353`)

Autonomous sessions can now file structured decision requests (`tc decision ask`, `#2037`) bound to an exact prompt digest, with the Operator alone authorized to record the binding answer (`tc decision answer`). If an appointed Architect or PM becomes unreachable with unread mail and an expired workload receipt (`#2351`), TangleClaw automatically raises a fallback alert. And when a long-running decider passes 8 hours of runtime or drops to 12% context (`#2353`), TangleClaw schedules a governed refresh alert that politely waits until active work and owed replies finish.

3. Native Codex Transcript Capture (`#2074`) & Coordinator-Rotation Hardening (`#2034`)

Wrapping a Codex session now captures its native rollout transcript from `~/.codex/sessions/**/*.jsonl` (with secret scanning and an idempotent `backfillTranscript` helper for previously wrapped sessions, `#2074`). Meanwhile, `PR #2827` (`#2034`) closed all 8 follow-ups from the `#2032` coordinator-rotation review—aligning `currentGeneration()` with the epoch gate, surfacing git/filesystem fingerprint errors, pruning stale rotation rows safely, and enforcing route-action declarations across every gated endpoint.
