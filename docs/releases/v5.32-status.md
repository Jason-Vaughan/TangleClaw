## Not verified, and said so

- **The #2268 banner has been seen in a browser by nobody.** The live host's tmux global environment holds no identity variable, and nobody plants one. It stands on its tests.
- **The #2272 outage and recovery notices have not been raised on the live install.** Raising one means holding the live helper off Discord for five minutes. They stand on their tests.
- **The #2272 reconnect logic is not proved against Discord itself**, which cannot be made to stall on demand. It is proved against Node's real WebSocket and servers that refuse, accept and say nothing, or greet and say nothing.
- **#2273's `deliveryWarning` reaches the API response and the server log only.** No screen shows it; that is #2293.
- **Screens accepted on tests only, carried from batch 2:** #2178 states 1 and 3; #2197 screen 11, the rule-changing buttons of screen 13, screen 5 at 375 px, and screens 15 to 18. #2284's wizard sentence likewise.
