# v6.1.0 — Bounded self-healing, operating profiles, wave scheduler, grounded assistants and knowledge base

TangleClaw v6.1.0 adds the v6.1 core modules (bounded recovery, operating profiles, standing failover and quota-aware placement, the wave scheduler, open-weight model qualification, the settings assistant, grounded report Q&A, a version-aware knowledge base, code-to-doc parity, a scrubbed support bundle, and educational walkthroughs) and mounts them under `/api/v6/*` with Fleet Shell bindings.

## Included

- **Bounded self-healing remediator** (#2991, A2b): three pre-authorized, non-destructive repairs only, with an atomic per-incident attempt budget and a four-state recovery circuit that only an Operator can unlatch.
- **Operating profiles** (#2992, A3): `Full` / `Lean` / `Minimal` over an immutable safety floor no override can disable, plus a benchmark aggregator that reports only measured samples.
- **Wave scheduler** (#1886, C3): dependency waves, whole-file-scope reservation across runs, and dependents held until an independent reviewer's gate passes.
- **Standing failover policy and quota-aware placement** (#2993, C4+D2): confirmation stays the default, and only an Operator-ratified, versioned policy may auto-switch. Every decision records a durable receipt.
- **Open-weight qualification harness** (#2994, D3): six gates per fleet role for local Ollama/vLLM models. An unmeasured metric fails its gate.
- **Settings assistant** (#2995, B3): a catalog of explained settings, a scope override inspector, and a preview/commit flow that commits exactly the previewed diff, once.
- **Grounded "Ask This Report"** (#2996, E6b): a synthesized answer is returned only when every sentence cites a retrieved report passage. Otherwise the verbatim cited passages are returned instead.
- **Version-aware knowledge base** (#2997, F1): budgeted, section-level search and contextual help over the product manual, with `page#anchor` citations tagged by version.
- **Code-to-doc parity checker** (#2998, F2): generates the CLI, route, project-default and engine reference tables from code, reports drift as a line diff, and fails closed on anything it cannot read with certainty.
- **Support diagnostic bundle** (#2999, F3): an allowlisted, scrubbed and verified bundle, filed only with consent bound to its preview digest.
- **Educational walkthroughs** (#3000, H2): five walkthroughs and an architecture glossary behind a publication gate. All five ship unverified.
- **Live routes and shell views** (#1886, #2991-#3000, Car V61-W2-ROUTES): 31 operator-only `/api/v6/*` routes expose all of the above. The v6 Fleet Shell adds a Settings & Profiles view (`#/settings/`), contextual help from the shipped manual (`#/settings/help`), and a per-project Recovery Console (`#/settings/recovery/<id>`).
