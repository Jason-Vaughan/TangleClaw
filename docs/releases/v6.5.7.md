# v6.5.7 — Under the Hood traffic filter, scroll pinning, and multi-tab fleet sync

TangleClaw v6.5.7 is a patch release on top of v6.5.6. Under the Hood now hides Medusa & fleet chatter by default, with a filter that stays applied across polls and reopens; its scrollable panes (and the main chat stream) stay pinned to the newest line across polls, link unfurls and reopening the drawer instead of drifting from it; and a fleet filter chosen in one browser tab no longer leaks into another tab's sidebar once that tab reloads. Milestone [#93](https://github.com/Jason-Vaughan/TangleClaw/milestone/93): 2 of 2 issues closed.

## Fixed

- **UTH traffic filter, scroll pinning, multi-tab fleet sync** (#3191): Under the Hood surfaced every Medusa/switchboard message by default, drowning out fleet chatter an operator actually wanted tucked away. Adds a "Hide Medusa & fleet chatter" checkbox (`#v6ChatHideHoodTraffic`, checked by default) that filters it out of the raw pane too via a new pure `filterPeekTrafficLines` helper (`tcV6FilterPeekTrafficLines`), not just the tucked-away list. Every scrollable pane (stream, peek, switchboard, tucked, and the drawer itself) now stays pinned to its newest line across polls, redraws, and reopening — a new `peekPinned` flag (mirroring `streamPinned`) with upward-scroll-only unpin tracking replaces a one-shot check that read as unpinned whenever the drawer was closed or had just resized, and a genuinely scrolled-up pane's exact position survives a full render via `captureViewState`/`restoreViewState`. A new `activeLogicalProjectId()` also reconciles the Fleet sidebar's `selectedGroups` back to the active conversation's own fleet, so another tab's `tc_v6_selected_groups` write no longer leaves this tab's sidebar on the wrong fleet after a reload.
- **UTH traffic toggle styling** (#3192): styled the "Hide Medusa & fleet chatter" toggle (`.v6-chat-verbosity-option--traffic`) as a distinct pill, set apart from the chat-reply verbosity checkboxes by a divider and a brand-accent tint, and added `.v6-chat-under-hood.is-traffic-hidden .v6-chat-tucked`/`.v6-chat-switchboard`/`.v6-chat-tucked-count` plus `[hidden]` rules so a hidden Medusa/fleet-chatter section takes zero vertical space inside Under the Hood rather than reserving a gap.
