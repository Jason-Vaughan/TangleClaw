# v6.5.3 — Charter & Roadmap release classification, Kaizen reports catalog, full-width chat, and Wizard/UTH fixes

TangleClaw v6.5.3 is a minor release on top of v6.5.2. The `📜 Charter & Roadmap` panel now classifies every already-shipped release correctly instead of showing all of history as upcoming; `📈 Kaizen & Reports` gets a real reports catalog and release-commit/PR/issue KPIs for a shipped sprint instead of empty placeholders; the v6 chat stream now spans the full center-pane width with a universal 4-engine "Working under the hood…" busy indicator and a fixed multi-choice false-positive; the Fleet Setup Wizard stops rendering twice; and Under the Hood keeps its full scrollback instead of truncating assistant replies when collapsed. Alongside these, the wave scheduler's `createdAt` no longer reads "Invalid Date" and new tmux sessions get a wide `200x50` starting window. Milestone [#88](https://github.com/Jason-Vaughan/TangleClaw/milestone/88): 6 of 6 issues closed.

## Changed

- **Full-width chat stream and busy pills** (#3167): `.v6-conversation-stream`, `.v6-conversation-event`, and `.v6-conversation-content` now span the full center-pane width and wrap long paragraphs instead of clipping to a narrower column. `#v6ChatActivity`'s busy pill gained a rotating `⟳` spinner and brand-accent styling, with a matching `.v6-uth-status--busy` badge for the Under the Hood summary bar.

## Fixed

- **Charter & Roadmap release classification** (#3162): `GET /api/sprint-waves` now derives `shippedVersions` from `CHANGELOG.md`'s released headings, `docs/releases/v*.md`, and `version.json`, and passes it into `buildSprintWaveSnapshot()` so a shipped version — and its synthesized fallback rows for a release with no dispatched car — renders under **Released Versions** with `Completed` pills instead of **Upcoming Planned Releases**.
- **Wave scheduler `createdAt` and wide tmux geometry** (#3163): `SqliteSchedulerStore#read()` backfills `createdAt` from the SQLite `created_at` column when a wave run's own `state_json` omits it, fixing "Invalid Date" in the Sprint & Cars UI. New tmux sessions now start at `200x50` instead of tmux's own `80x24` default, and `ensureMinWindowWidth` restores a session's window to `200x50` whenever a narrow mobile/browser client has shrunk it below 120 columns.
- **Kaizen & Reports catalog and KPIs** (#3164): a completed car's recorded PR now reports as `merged` instead of staying `open` forever once the car was actually done. `GET /api/sprint-waves` and `GET /api/sprint-reports/:version` now populate the Sprint Reports Hub catalog (from `docs/releases/v*.md` and `v*-status.md`, plus hub-linked retrospectives) and the real release commit, and every car carries normalized top-level `carId`/`branch`/`title`/`status` fields.
- **Fleet Setup Wizard no longer renders twice** (#3165): the Wizard and Model Attestation card now render exclusively inside the `🧙 Wizard` tab, not also below an idle `#/overview` screen in Chat mode.
- **Under the Hood keeps full scrollback when collapsed** (#3166): the session peek poll now reads the full 2000-line deep scrollback unconditionally, instead of 80 lines while the drawer is closed, so assistant replies no longer vanish from the chat stream. The `⌨ Interactive` toggle's tooltip now says explicitly it types into Under the Hood's own live pane. The roadmap sync status check also now fails closed on a stale snapshot or a shipped release missing its hub link, instead of silently reporting "ok".
- **Universal busy indicator and chat reflow** (#3167): the "Working under the hood…" busy indicator now covers all 4 engines (Claude Code, Antigravity, Codex, Aider), recognizing each engine's own busy markers. `extractMultiChoice` no longer renders a declarative numbered list as a bogus multi-choice card. `extractLiveTurns` unwraps soft-wrapped continuation lines into flowing paragraphs instead of force-wrapping replies to the terminal's column width, while preserving real paragraph breaks, headings, list items and code fences.
