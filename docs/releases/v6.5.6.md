# v6.5.6 — Two-way Gobble & Fill chat pane resizers and a sticky Under the Hood toggle

TangleClaw v6.5.6 is a patch release on top of v6.5.5. The V6 chat window gets 4 accessible drag/keyboard resizer bars that trade height 1:1 and zero-sum ("Gobble & Fill") between whichever of Main Chat, Under the Hood, and the composer are actually open, and Under the Hood's `🔧` toggle bar now stays pinned to the top of its own scroll container instead of scrolling out of reach while the drawer is open. Milestone [#92](https://github.com/Jason-Vaughan/TangleClaw/milestone/92): 2 of 2 issues closed.

## Fixed

- **Gobble & Fill chat pane resizers** (#3186): the V6 chat window's panes had no way to trade space with each other — only `#v6ChatUnderHood`/`#v6ChatPeek`/`#v6ChatInput`'s own tiny native `resize: vertical` grips existed, and Main Chat had none at all. Adds 4 accessible drag/keyboard resizer bars at the Main Chat / Under the Hood / Composer boundaries (`tradeChatPaneHeights`, `chatPaneResizerMarkup`) that trade height 1:1 and zero-sum between whichever panes are open, with a joint viewport budget so neither Under the Hood nor the composer can together push Main Chat or its own controls off-screen.
- **Sticky Under the Hood toggle bar and resizer styling** (#3187): `.v6-chat-under-hood > summary` is now `position: sticky; top: 0`, pinned to the top of `.v6-chat-under-hood[open]`'s own scroll container (with an opaque surface fill so scrolled content doesn't show through), so the collapse toggle and its Full Screen/Interactive buttons stay reachable regardless of how tall or scrolled the drawer is. Also adds `.v6-chat-pane-resizer`/`.v6-chat-pane-resizer-grip` styling — venom-green left/center/right grips on hover, keyboard focus, and drag — for the new two-way drag handles from #3186.
