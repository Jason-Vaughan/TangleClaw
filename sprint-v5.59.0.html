📖 Plain-English Summary — What Sprint v5.59.0 Accomplished & Why It Matters Written for operators, stakeholders & non-specialist readers

3-Tier Remote Operator Authority, Persistent Morpheus Advisor Role, Multi-Channel Bridge Reach & Advisory-Only AI-to-AI Discord Room

As operators coordinate multi-agent fleets remotely through Discord and the TangleClaw dashboard, two critical boundaries are needed: (1) high-blast-radius remote actions must require explicit, tamper-proof confirmation by the authorized operator, and (2) AI agents and external advisors must be able to exchange ideas and cross-project insights without accidentally triggering operational commands or escaping their sandbox. Sprint v5.59.0 (“Bridge authority and reach”, Leg 3 of our 5-version autonomous sprint train) delivered both boundaries across 8 shipped issues and 5 pull requests (+6,514 / -77 feature LOC):

1. 3-Tier Bridge Authority (`#2106, #2116`) & Advisory-Only `#ai-to-ai-chatroom` (`#2817, #2818`)

Car 59-C (PR #2850, lib/bridge-confirmation.js) classifies remote bridge commands into 3 tiers and requires single-use SHA-256 hashed-nonce confirmation cards verified against operator identity and payload version before Tier 3 actions run. Meanwhile, Car 59-A (PR #2859) adds an advisory-only Discord channel mode (#ai-to-ai-chatroom) with allowlists, sensitive-pattern scrubbing, and a 6-turn loop circuit breaker that refuses any execution authority.

2. Persistent Morpheus `🕶️ Advisor` Role, Control-Bar Drawer & Isolated Sandbox (`#2819`)

Car 59-B (PR #2849, +2,642 / -1 LOC in lib/advisor.js and public/*) introduces the persistent Morpheus 🕶️ Advisor global role with its own dashboard drawer, isolated ~/.tangleclaw/advisor-sandbox/ filesystem boundary (rejecting symlink escapes and absolute Glob patterns), and server-minted launch-ID authentication.

3. Group-Scoped Bridge Reach, Session Awareness & Multi-Channel Watch Grants (`#2117, #2119, #2394`)

Car 59-D (PR #2848, +1,671 / -7 LOC across lib/bridge-reach.js, lib/bridge-awareness.js, and lib/bridge-channels.js) adds group-scoped notification reach with batch opt-out, session-aware notification routing policy, and a multi-channel watch inventory with explicit read/write channel grants.

What Didn't Work & Concrete Guardrail Fixes Enacted in v5.59.0 → v5.60.0

1. Absolute Glob Patterns Could Bypass Base-Path Sandbox Checks in Morpheus Advisor (#2849 Round 2)

In `Car 59-B` (`PR #2849`), checking only that the `path` argument resolved inside `~/.tangleclaw/advisor-sandbox/` was insufficient if a caller passed an absolute `pattern` to Glob. `74b5f99d` added explicit rejection of absolute or `..`-traversing Glob patterns. Fix / Guardrail: Any sandboxed file-search tool must validate both the base directory and the pattern string against absolute paths and parent-directory traversal.

2. Self-Asserted Role Claims in Request Headers Must Never Grant Role Privileges (#2849 Round 1)

Initial Advisor route checks inspected a client-supplied role claim before `e8640ede` bound `isAdvisorRequest` in `lib/routes/_callers.js` strictly to the server-recorded launch ID. Fix / Rule: All role-specific route guards (`PM`, `Master`, `Advisor`, `Builder`) must derive caller role exclusively from the server-side session/launch registry.

3. Premature Nonce Consumption Before Operator/Version Verification (#2850 Round 1)

In `Car 59-C` (`PR #2850`), consuming a confirmation card nonce before checking whether the caller matched the card's target operator allowed a mismatched caller to invalidate a pending card. `bdb415ce` reordered verification before state mutation. Fix / Guardrail: Single-use token/lease consumers must validate actor identity and version preconditions prior to mutating token state.

4. Cross-PR `.testament.toml` & `test/tiers.json` Contention Across 4 Concurrent Wave 1 Cars (#2848)

Because all 4 `v5.59.0` cars registered new test files in `.testament.toml` and `test/tiers.json`, merging `#2850` and `#2849` required a fast ancestor sync on `#2848` (`80de9508`) before merge. Fix / Guardrail: `TC-Lane-INT` merges `origin/main` into each queued PR branch whenever `.testament.toml` or `test/tiers.json` moves on `main`.
